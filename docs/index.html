Why use Behave?

Ditch the pre-exam cramming. Study smarter by reviewing only what your brain is starting to forget.

📈

Fibonacci Scheduling

Dynamically schedules reviews at optimal mathematical intervals (+5h, +2d, +3d, +5d, +8d). Recalling notes pushes reviews further away; forgetting them reviews them sooner.

📸

Visual Notes & Markdown

Write structured study notes with rich Markdown or capture quick visual pages directly using the integrated custom camera tool to embed snaps instantly.

⏰

Offline System Alarms

Notifications are managed entirely client-side using Android's exact alarm scheduler. No battery drain, runs offline, and automatically reschedules itself after phone reboots.

Why does Android show a "Block Harmful App" warning?

Since this APK is compiled directly from source and is not downloaded from the Google Play Store, Android Play Protect doesn't recognize the signature. Tap "Install anyway" to proceed safely.
