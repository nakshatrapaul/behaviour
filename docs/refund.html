Refund Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

At Behave, we want you to be completely satisfied with your premium spaced repetition experience. We design and build our products to the highest standards, but we recognize that sometimes the service may not meet every user's individual needs. Therefore, we offer a straightforward, risk-free refund guarantee.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We provide a full, no-questions-asked refund within 14 days of your initial purchase date. If you purchased a Behave Premium license or subscription and decide it's not the right fit for your studies, you are eligible for a complete reimbursement.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request your refund, please follow these steps:

  • Send an email to our support desk at nakshatrapaul4@gmail.com.
  • Use the subject line: "Refund Request - Behave Premium".
  • Include the receipt, invoice, or checkout email confirmation you received from our payment merchant (Paddle). If you don't have the receipt, please provide the email address used during purchase and the Paddle Transaction ID (if known).

3. Refund Processing & Timelines

Once we receive your request, we will process the refund with our merchant partner, Paddle, within 24 to 48 hours.

  • After processing, Paddle will return the funds to your original payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
  • It typically takes 3 to 10 business days for the refunded amount to appear on your bank statement or card balance, depending on your financial institution.
  • Your Behave Premium features will be automatically downgraded to the Free tier once the refund is completed.

4. Subscription Cancellation

If you purchased a recurring subscription and wish to prevent future charges rather than requesting a refund for a past payment, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Every payment invoice email you receive from Paddle contains a direct cancellation link. Alternatively, email us at nakshatrapaul4@gmail.com and we will assist you with the cancellation.

5. Contact Us

For any other inquiries, support tickets, or clarification regarding payments, billing, or refunds, please visit our Contact Page or write directly to us at nakshatrapaul4@gmail.com.
