Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

At Behave, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the security measures we take to protect it. By using Behave, you consent to the data collection and practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Behave is designed to minimize the collection of personal data. To provide you with spaced repetition scheduling and note synchronization, we collect and store the following:

  • Study Notes & Media: We store the notes, cards, and camera image attachments you create inside the App. This data is stored locally on your device and, if cloud sync is enabled, is backed up securely on our backend database.
  • Account Credentials: If you register an account to back up and sync your notes, we store your username/email and a hashed version of your password. We do not store raw, readable passwords.
  • Analytics Data: We may collect generic, anonymous usage statistics to help us debug crash issues and improve app scheduling performance. This does not contain any readable note contents or identifiable user details.

2. Payment & Transactions

All premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by Paddle, our third-party Merchant of Record. Paddle is responsible for collecting and processing credit card details, VAT/taxes, billing addresses, and other transaction-related details.

Paddle has its own privacy policy governing transaction data. Behave does not collect, receive, or store any sensitive payment information on our servers.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers to power features of the App:

  • Appwrite: Used as our cloud backend database and hosting service for note synchronization. All sync data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Paddle: Handles all billing, licensing, and transaction verification processes.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, notes, or contact information with any other third parties or advertisers.

4. Security of Your Data

We apply robust administrative and technical controls to secure your data. Notes are stored in your private user space. Our sync backend enforces strict authentication rules, ensuring that only you can access, download, or edit your synchronized study sets.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your study notes and credentials for as long as you maintain your Behave account. You can delete individual notes or study sets at any time from within the App. If you would like to permanently delete your entire Behave account and all associated cloud sync backups, you can do so through the account settings in the App, or by contacting us at nakshatrapaul4@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Behave does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Page or write to us at nakshatrapaul4@gmail.com.
